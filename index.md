# PeriodSaathi Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 15, 2026

**Contact:** sleepyowl47@gmail.com

## 1. Introduction

PeriodSaathi is a menstrual-cycle tracking application that helps users record and review period dates, flow, symptoms, notes, cycle preferences, fertility estimates, and reminder settings.

This policy explains how the app handles information and the choices available to users.

## 2. Information the App Handles

Depending on how you use PeriodSaathi, the app may store:

- Period dates and daily period records.
- Flow information, symptoms, and notes you enter.
- Cycle-length and irregular-cycle preferences.
- Reminder settings and onboarding preferences.
- Your preferred app language.

PeriodSaathi may calculate estimated ovulation dates and fertile windows using your logged period history. These are estimates, not confirmed medical findings.

## 3. Local Storage and Processing

Period history and app preferences are stored locally on your Android device using the app's local storage.

Fertility estimates are calculated from period history available within the app. The app may withhold estimates when there is insufficient information or substantial cycle variability.

A review of the app's source code did not identify app code that sends period dates, flow information, symptoms, notes, cycle preferences, or reminder settings to a remote server. The reviewed project did not contain identified analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or attribution SDKs.

This describes the reviewed version and does not guarantee that future versions or every component of the Android operating system will have identical data practices.

Your information remains within the app's local storage unless you choose to export or share it, or otherwise make it available outside the app.

## 4. Notifications

If you enable reminders and grant the required permission, PeriodSaathi schedules local notifications on your device. The reviewed notification text does not include your personal period dates, symptoms, or notes.

The app name and notification message may reveal that you use a period-tracking app. Whether notifications appear on your lock screen depends on your Android device and notification settings.

You can disable reminders in the app settings or manage notification permissions through Android.

## 5. Fertility and Ovulation Estimates

PeriodSaathi may display estimated ovulation dates and fertile windows based on logged period history and the app's prediction method.

These estimates can be inaccurate. Calendar-based predictions cannot confirm whether ovulation has occurred or will occur on a particular date. Estimates may be withheld when there is insufficient history or substantial cycle variability.

**Do not rely on PeriodSaathi's predictions to prevent pregnancy or identify guaranteed safe days.** The app is intended for informational and tracking purposes, not as a contraceptive method, diagnostic tool, or substitute for professional medical advice.

For advice about fertility, contraception, menstrual irregularities, or reproductive health, consult a qualified healthcare professional.

## 6. Exporting and Sharing

PeriodSaathi allows you to export period history as a CSV file. The export may include period dates, daily records, flow information, symptoms, and notes.

You may save the file to a location you select or share it using Android's sharing interface. A destination app or service may store, transmit, or sync the file under its own privacy practices. PeriodSaathi does not control how another app handles a file you choose to share.

Keep exported files secure and share them only with people or services you trust.

## 7. Data Deletion and Backups

PeriodSaathi provides a **Delete All Data** feature that removes period history and the specified cycle and reminder preferences stored by the app.

The app's language preference and onboarding-completion preference may remain after this action. The feature does not delete CSV files previously exported or shared, or copies stored by other apps or services.

The Android backup configuration excludes the app's SharedPreferences file from the configured cloud-backup and device-transfer rules. Device manufacturers and transfer mechanisms may behave differently.

## 8. Third-Party Services

The reviewed source code and dependencies did not identify analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or attribution SDKs, and no app-level code sending period records to a remote server was found.

The app uses Android's file-selection and sharing mechanisms when you export or share information. The destination app you select may process the information you provide to it.

This policy concerns the PeriodSaathi app. Other apps, operating-system services, and websites have their own data practices.

## 9. Security

PeriodSaathi uses Android application storage for locally stored information and preferences. The reviewed implementation does not add application-level encryption to the stored preferences.

Android's application sandbox helps restrict access to an app's private storage, but no method of storage can guarantee absolute security. An unlocked or compromised device, or privileged system access, may create additional risks.

Protect access to your device and take care when exporting or sharing personal health information.

## 10. Audience and Children's Privacy

PeriodSaathi is intended to provide menstrual-cycle tracking and educational information to users who may benefit from these features.

The developer must establish the app's intended age groups and comply with applicable laws and platform requirements. The app's actual target audience and any additional safeguards must be reflected accurately in the relevant app-store declarations.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will be available at the public privacy-policy URL used in the app's store listing.
