# PeriodSaathi Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 10, 2026

**Contact:** sleepyowl47@gmail.com

## 1. Introduction

PeriodSaathi is a menstrual-cycle tracking application that helps users record and review period dates, flow, symptoms, notes, cycle preferences, and reminder settings.

This policy explains how the app handles information and the choices available to users.

## 2. Information the App Handles

Depending on how you use PeriodSaathi, the app may store:

- Period dates and daily period records.
- Flow information, symptoms, and notes you enter.
- Cycle-length preferences.
- Reminder and onboarding preferences.

## 3. Local Storage

Period history and app preferences are stored locally on your Android device using the app's local storage.

A review of the current source code did not identify app code that sends period dates, flow information, symptoms, notes, cycle preferences, or reminder settings to a remote server. The reviewed project did not contain identified analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or attribution SDKs.

This statement reflects the reviewed version. Future app versions may have different data practices.

## 4. Notifications

If you enable reminders and grant the required permission, PeriodSaathi schedules local notifications on your device. The reviewed notification text does not include personal period details.

You can disable reminders in the app settings or manage notification permissions through Android.

## 5. Exporting and Sharing

PeriodSaathi allows you to export period history as a CSV file. The export may include period dates, flow information, symptoms, and notes.

You may save the file to a location you select or share it using Android's sharing interface. A destination app or service may store, transmit, or sync the file under its own privacy practices. PeriodSaathi does not control how another app handles a file you choose to share.

## 6. Data Deletion and Backups

PeriodSaathi provides a **Delete All Data** feature that removes the period history and specified cycle and reminder preferences stored by the app.

The Android backup configuration excludes the app's SharedPreferences file from the configured cloud-backup and device-transfer rules. Device manufacturers and transfer mechanisms may behave differently.

Deleting data in PeriodSaathi does not delete CSV files previously exported or shared.

## 7. Third-Party Services

The reviewed source code and dependencies did not identify analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or attribution SDKs, and no app-level code sending period records to a remote server was found.

The app uses Android's file-selection and sharing mechanisms when you export or share information. The destination app you select may process the information you provide to it.

## 8. Security

PeriodSaathi uses Android application storage for locally stored information and preferences. No method of storage can guarantee absolute security. Protect access to your device and take care when exporting or sharing personal health information.

## 9. Children's Privacy

The developer must establish the app's intended age group and comply with applicable requirements. The app's availability to children, if any, will be governed by applicable laws and platform policies.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will be available at the public privacy-policy URL used in the app's store listing.
